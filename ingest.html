Paste an interview transcript or a set of insights. You review every card before anything is added to the corpus.

The corpus is only as good as what goes into it. Every card here becomes evidence that future verdicts cite, so a card that overstates what someone said quietly corrupts them. Read the wording before you add it — that is what this step is for. Similarity checking is heuristic keyword matching, not embeddings: it flags likely overlaps but misses paraphrases with no shared vocabulary, so a card it doesn't flag isn't necessarily new.
